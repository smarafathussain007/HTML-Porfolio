About Me

I’m S M Arafat Hussain, a Web Developer and creative professional from Khulna, Bangladesh. I’m passionate about building modern, responsive, and user-friendly websites that combine clean design with practical functionality. Alongside web development, I have experience in UI/UX Design, Graphic Design, Branding, and Digital Marketing, which helps me approach websites from both a technical and creative perspective. I’m also the CEO of Detective Designers, where I work on digital and creative projects for businesses and brands.

My interest in web development goes beyond simply writing code. I enjoy understanding how a website looks, feels, and works from the user’s perspective. I like turning ideas and designs into responsive websites and continuously improving my skills by working on real projects and exploring new technologies. My background in design and digital marketing also allows me to think about user experience, visual communication, performance, and the overall purpose of a website—not just the code behind it.

I’m a self-driven learner who believes that skills grow through practice, experimentation, and real-world experience. I’m continuously developing my knowledge in web development while expanding my abilities across design and digital technologies. My goal is to become a well-rounded developer who can transform ideas into useful, engaging, and professional digital experiences. I’m always open to learning, taking on new challenges, and building something meaningful with technology.
